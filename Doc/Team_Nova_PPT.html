What Is IN-OUT?

IN-OUT is a campus-deployed intelligence platform that replaces manual hostel leave registers and paper gate passes with a live, role-driven system. Wardens see requests the moment they are submitted. Gate security verifies cryptographic QR passes on exit. Parents receive an automated message the instant their ward leaves or returns — with zero reliance on the internet.

Real-World Domain
EdTech Administration — The Frontier Nobody Digitised
IN-OUT operates at the intersection of institutional governance, real-time systems engineering, and campus safety infrastructure.

EdTech Administration

Automating the governance layer of residential education — wardens, students, and security operating on a single unified platform instead of three separate paper trails.

Real-Time Systems Engineering

Event-driven architecture using WebSockets ensures zero-latency decision communication. A warden's approval reaches a student's screen in under a second.

Campus Safety Infrastructure

Role-based access, cryptographic QR verification, automated overdue alerts, and immutable audit logs make IN-OUT a safety-critical institutional tool.

Why Not Cloud?

Campus intranet delivers 100% uptime with zero monthly cost and full data sovereignty on premises.

Why Not an App?

A progressive web platform works on any device — no installation, no app store delays.

Why WebSockets?

HTTP polling wastes bandwidth. Socket.IO pushes events instantly, keeping the warden dashboard always live.

Why TypeScript?

Shared types between client and server eliminate data mismatch bugs before deployment.

System Architecture of the Problem
The Broken Chain in Hostel Governance
Every existing hostel process has a structural failure point. Here is where the chain breaks — and where IN-OUT intervenes.
Stage 01

Paper Application

Student writes a form. Warden may not be available for hours.

Stage 02

Manual Approval

No digital queue. Emergency requests look identical to routine ones.

Stage 03

Paper Gate Pass

Guards verify nothing. Passes can be forged, copied, or reused.

Stage 04

No Return Signal

Overdue students go undetected until a manual roll call.

Outcome

Zero Accountability

No audit trail. No parent alert. No record of what happened or when.

Without IN-OUT

  • Hours to approve a leave request
  • Paper passes with no verification
  • Parents uninformed until they call
  • No automated overdue detection
  • Disputes with no evidence trail

The IN-OUT Resolution

A unified platform where a student submits a request, the warden sees it instantly via WebSocket push, approves it in one action, the gate receives a cryptographic QR verification link, the parent receives an SMS at the moment of exit, and the system autonomously triggers an overdue alert — with every event timestamped and permanently logged.

Literature Survey
Research That Shaped Our Thinking
Four baseline papers — who built it, what they did, and how IN-OUT advances each one.
Baseline 01

Smart Hostel Management System Using IoT and Web Technologies

R. Karthik, S. Murugan et al. — IJRASET, Vol. 9, April 2021
What They Did

RFID-based entry logging with a basic web dashboard showing gate scan records.

What We Improved

Added warden approval workflow, parent SMS alerts, and a full 9-state request lifecycle absent in their system.

View Paper
Baseline 02

Real-Time Student Attendance Management System Using QR Code

P. Sharma, A. Jain — IRJET, Vol. 7, Issue 5, May 2020
What They Did

Static QR codes assigned to students for classroom attendance scanning.

What We Improved

Request-bound QR codes that expire after single use, cryptographically tied to a specific approved leave — making forgery structurally impossible.

View Paper
Baseline 03

Role-Based Access Control in Web Applications — A Survey

E. Bertino, P. Sandhu — IEEE Access, Vol. 8, 2020
What They Did

A theoretical survey of RBAC patterns and JWT-based permission models for multi-actor systems.

What We Improved

Implemented strict API-level role guards for all four roles, enforced by JWT middleware and Zod schema validation, preventing privilege escalation.

View Paper
Baseline 04

Real-Time Notification System Using WebSockets for Institutional Use

V. Kumar, R. Patel — IJCSE, Vol. 11, Issue 3, 2023
What They Did

A generic WebSocket notification layer for campus event broadcasting without authentication.

What We Improved

Authenticated every Socket.IO connection with the same JWT used by the REST API, closing the snooping vulnerability on shared campus LAN environments.

View Paper
